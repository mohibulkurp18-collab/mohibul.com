From an idea on paper to work on the ground.

Large datasets, made trustworthy and useful.

I manage, validate, analyse and visualise large-scale spatial and non-spatial datasets, from nationwide BBS census and demographic data to telecom network data, and present them as maps, dashboards, reports and presentations for government agencies, clients and decision-makers.

03 · GIS & REMOTE SENSING

Raster and vector: from pixels and polygons to plans.

I work on both sides of GIS: raster data from satellite and drone imagery, and vector data (points, lines and polygons) for overlay, proximity and network analysis. Both meet in one geodatabase and end as maps decision-makers can use.
