THE ROUTE SO FAR

Eleven stops, year by year

A timeline of where I was and what I did. Each bar is coloured by the discipline that led there.

01

1998 — 2016 · Gaibandha, Nilphamari, Saidpur

Roots in the north

I was born in Gaibandha, in northern Bangladesh. From 1998 to 2011 I grew up in Nilphamari and Saidpur, and from 2012 to 2016 I was back home in Gaibandha. In 2017 I moved south to Khulna, and my work has since taken me across the country.

02

2018 — 2023 · Khulna

PMDataGIS

Dashed = foundations laid

Learning to read places

At Khulna University I studied for a Bachelor in Urban and Rural Planning. Planning taught me to see a place as a system of people, land, movement and services, and to back every proposal with evidence. My dissertation, Spatial Pattern of Travel Behavior: A Comparative Case Study, was my first deep dive into combining spatial analysis with data.

03

2022 — 2023 · Jashore, Khulna, Dhaka, Satkhira

PMDataGIS

Into the field

Before graduating I was already collecting data on the ground. For the Housing & Building Research Institute I surveyed brickfields across the Jashore–Khulna region, studying how the sector could move to lower-emission, non-fired technology. At CEGIS I digitised features from satellite and scanned images to build a mouza-based vector dataset for a Bagerhat upazila. And in Satkhira, on the GIZ-funded Climate Resilient Inclusive Smart Cities project, I monitored public hearings and turned what communities said into the final draft report.

04

2023 — 2024 · Khulna → Dhaka

PMDataGIS

Data at scale

At Geo Smart Planning in Khulna, I became the person who held projects together, leading coordination and final documentation for four projects while drafting technical and financial proposals. Then CEGIS brought me back as a Research Consultant, where I co-led the Mouza and Plot-Based Land Zoning project: satellite imagery, plot-level vector data and validated ground control points became digital land-use and crop-pattern datasets for 19 upazilas in 6 districts of Khulna Division.

05

2024 — 2026 · Dhaka & nationwide

PMDataGIS

Delivering nationwide

In April 2024 I joined Fiber@Home Ltd. as Engineer – Project & Data Management. It was the role where all three disciplines finally worked as one. On Connected Bangladesh I coordinated the design, planning and implementation of network infrastructure for 408 unions. On Social Liberty 2.0 I led the set-up of 16 Digital Public Service Centres. For a 12.5 MWp rooftop solar project in the DESCO area, I ran the solar radiation and feasibility analysis.
