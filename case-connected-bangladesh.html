THE SCALE

One project. 408 unions.

Each dot is one administrative unit the project planned for. Unions are where connectivity actually reaches people.

The challenge

Large parts of rural Bangladesh sit in underserved telecommunication areas. This national project set out to establish broadband connectivity there, planning network infrastructure union by union, across 29 districts, with many agencies involved.

My role

As Project Coordinator at Fiber@Home Ltd., I coordinated the design, planning and implementation of the network infrastructure, acting as the link between internal teams, the client, consultants and government agencies.

APPROACH

How the three disciplines worked together

Project Management

  • Coordinated the project from design through planning to implementation
  • Kept internal teams, client, consultants and government agencies aligned
  • Tracked delivery against implementation plans and Gantt charts

Data Analysis & Management

  • Managed and validated the large spatial and non-spatial datasets behind the plan
  • Turned them into reports, dashboards and executive presentations

GIS & Remote Sensing

  • Vector analysis of union and district boundaries in ArcGIS and QGIS
  • Mapped union-level coverage to support network planning and optimisation

RESULTS

Network infrastructure planned for 408 unions.

Across 111 upazilas in 29 districts, over a two-year coordination role.
